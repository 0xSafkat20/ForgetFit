# ForgetFit FAQ and Blog Content Plan

## Goal

Help prospective members answer practical questions before visiting, then support beginners with useful articles that build confidence and demonstrate the gym's coaching approach.

## FAQ structure

- **Your first visit:** what to bring, beginner support, changing rooms, and how the free pass works.
- **Membership details:** pausing a membership and cancellation notice.
- The same source content is reused on the Membership page and the dedicated FAQ page, so updates remain consistent.
- Each answer is displayed in an accessible expandable panel using native browser controls.

## Starter blog series

1. **Your first week at the gym** — removes first-visit uncertainty and gives readers a manageable first-week checklist.
2. **Strength training for beginners** — introduces movement patterns, effort, simple programming, progression, and safety.
3. **How to build a consistent routine** — explains scheduling, reducing friction, tracking attendance, and returning after missed sessions.
4. **What to expect from a coached session** — sets expectations for the intake conversation, coaching process, feedback, and next steps.
5. **How gym orientation works** — explains the facility tour, equipment setup, etiquette, and the first independent session.

## Publishing and maintenance

- The articles are seeded once as published WordPress posts in the **Training Guides** category.
- After creation they are ordinary posts: editors can revise, unpublish, schedule, or add featured images without theme updates overwriting their work.
- Before a real launch, confirm the changing-room facilities, free-pass eligibility, pause rules, and cancellation notice against the gym's actual operations and signed membership agreement.
- Recommended next additions: original featured photography, author bios, internal links to matching programs, and quarterly content reviews.
